Harvest & Selection

Fresh, healthy green neem leaves (Azadirachta indica) handpicked and inspected.

2
Surface Washing

Thorough washing with purified water to remove adhering dust, dirt, and foreign botanical contaminants.

3
Cellular Maceration

Mechanical crushing of leaves to maximize surface area contact for efficient aqueous solvent diffusion.

4
Thermal Aqueous Extraction

Soaked in distilled water and heated to boiling to dissolve hydrophilic bioactive limonoids and polyphenols.

5
Controlled Cooling

Extract allowed to equilibrate to room ambient temperature (25°C) to prevent thermal degradation.

6
Sterile Filtration

Filtration through fine filter paper to yield a crystal-clear, greenish bioactive neem aqueous filtrate.
